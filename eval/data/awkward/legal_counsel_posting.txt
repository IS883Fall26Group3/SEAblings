Senior Legal Counsel

About the role
A national non-profit is hiring a Senior Legal Counsel to advise its leadership on constitutional
and civil-rights matters.

Responsibilities
- Advise the executive team on litigation risk and regulatory questions
- Draft and review contracts, policies and amicus briefs
- Manage outside counsel on active cases

Requirements
- J.D. degree from an accredited law school
- Admission to the bar in at least one U.S. state
- 5+ years of experience in litigation or constitutional law
- Excellent public speaking and writing skills
