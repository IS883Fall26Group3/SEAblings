Harbor & Pine Opens New Waterfront Office (written for this project's tests)

Harbor & Pine, a fictional design studio, celebrated the opening of its new waterfront office on
Friday. The two-floor space includes a rooftop garden, a public gallery and a cafe run by a local
bakery. "We wanted a place where the neighborhood feels welcome," said the studio's founder at the
ribbon cutting. The gallery's first exhibition, a collection of hand-drawn harbor maps, is open to
visitors on weekends through the end of the year.
