Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies (written for this project's tests)

Ingredients
- 225 g butter, browned and cooled
- 200 g brown sugar
- 100 g white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 280 g flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 300 g dark chocolate, chopped

Method
1. Whisk the browned butter with both sugars until smooth.
2. Beat in the eggs and vanilla.
3. Fold in the flour and baking soda, then the chocolate.
4. Chill the dough for 30 minutes.
5. Bake at 180 C for 11 minutes, until the edges are golden.
